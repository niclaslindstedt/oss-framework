---
type: Added
title: Audio
---

A new `audio` module: level-meter and spectrum arithmetic, PCM tools, dependency-free WAV and FLAC encoders (MP3 through the optional `audio/mp3` entry), a microphone capture with `useRecorder` over it, `usePlayer`, and the `LevelMeter`, `SpectrumBars` and `Waveform` components; plus the mic and transport icons.
