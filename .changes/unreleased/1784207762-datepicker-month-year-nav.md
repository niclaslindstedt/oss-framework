type: Added
title: Quick month & year picking in DatePicker
---

The `DatePicker` header caption now zooms out to a month grid, and the year there to a twelve-year page, so a far-off date is a couple of taps instead of a long month-by-month drag — all in-panel, so it survives the iOS PWA where a native date input dismisses its own popover on a month change.
